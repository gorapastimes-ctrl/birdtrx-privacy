🐦 BirdTRX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 May 2026  ·  GoRa Pastimes
The short version: BirdTRX stores all your data on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal information. No account is required.

1. Who We Are

BirdTRX is developed and published by GoRa Pastimes, an independent app developer based in Dunfermline, Scotland, United Kingdom. If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at gorapastimes@gmail.com.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

BirdTRX does not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your diary entries, photos, audio recordings, or notes
  • Your bird sighting records or life list
  • Your GPS location (beyond what you choose to store in your own entries)
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioural data
  • Device identifiers

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Everything you create in BirdTRX is stored locally on your device:

  • Diary entries — text, photos, voice notes, and bird song ID results are stored in a local SQLite database and your device's local storage
  • Life list — built automatically from your diary entries, stored locally
  • App settings — preferences such as theme, confidence threshold, and country filter are stored in your device's local storage
  • Bird ID model — the BirdNET model (~259 MB) is downloaded once and stored on your device. It processes audio entirely on-device with no data sent externally

4. Permissions We Request

BirdTRX requests the following Android permissions:

  • Microphone — used for bird song identification and voice notes. Bird song audio is processed entirely on-device. Voice notes may optionally be sent to OpenAI for transcription if you are a premium subscriber and choose to use the transcription feature
  • Camera — used only to take photos for diary entries. Photos are stored locally on your device
  • Location (approximate) — used optionally to record the location of diary entries and to find nearby birding sites via the eBird API. Your coordinates are sent to eBird solely to retrieve nearby hotspot information and are not stored by us. Location is never tracked in the background
  • Storage / Media — used to read photos from your gallery and to write diary exports and backups
  • Internet — used for: downloading the BirdNET model on first use; retrieving weather data; finding nearby birding sites via eBird; species research via Wikipedia, xeno-canto, and iNaturalist; reverse geocoding via OpenStreetMap; voice note transcription via OpenAI (premium feature); and Google Drive backup (if you choose to enable it)

5. Third-Party Services

BirdTRX optionally connects to the following third-party services:

  • eBird API (Cornell Lab of Ornithology) — used to find nearby birding hotspots, recent notable sightings, and recent species observations. Your approximate GPS coordinates are sent to retrieve results. eBird's privacy policy is available at birds.cornell.edu
  • Open-Meteo — used to retrieve local weather conditions for diary entries. Your approximate GPS coordinates are sent to retrieve weather data. Open-Meteo does not store personal data. Their policy is at open-meteo.com
  • OpenAI Whisper (premium feature) — if you are a premium subscriber and choose to transcribe a voice note, the audio recording is sent to OpenAI's servers for transcription. OpenAI does not use API audio submissions to train their models and does not retain audio beyond processing your request. OpenAI's privacy policy is at openai.com/privacy
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia — used in the Research screen to retrieve species descriptions and photos. No personal data is sent. Wikimedia's privacy policy is at foundation.wikimedia.org
  • xeno-canto — used in the Research screen to retrieve bird call reference recordings. No personal data is sent. xeno-canto's policy is at xeno-canto.org
  • iNaturalist — used in the Research screen to retrieve species photos as a fallback when Wikipedia does not have an image. No personal data is sent. iNaturalist's privacy policy is at inaturalist.org/pages/privacy
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — used to convert your GPS coordinates to a readable place name (e.g. "Cairneyhill") for diary entries. Your approximate coordinates are sent but not stored by the service. OpenStreetMap's privacy policy is at osmfoundation.org
  • Google Drive — if you choose to enable Google Drive backup (premium feature), your diary data is backed up to your own Google Drive account. We do not have access to your Google account or your backup files. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Billing — subscription payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details

6. Google Drive Backup

If you choose to back up your diary to Google Drive (available to premium subscribers), your data is stored in your own Google Drive account under a folder named BirdTRX. GoRa Pastimes has no access to this data. You can delete your backup at any time directly from Google Drive.

7. Subscription and Payments

BirdTRX is available on a free tier with limited features, and as a paid premium subscription (monthly or annual) with a free trial period. Premium features include unlimited Bird Song ID recordings, voice note transcription, and automatic Google Drive backup.

All billing is handled by Google Play. We do not process or store any payment information. Subscription management is done through your Google Play account settings.

8. Children's Privacy

BirdTRX is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used BirdTRX and you have concerns, please contact us at gorapastimes@gmail.com.

9. Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your diary is subject to your device's own security measures (screen lock, encryption, etc.). We recommend keeping your device software up to date and using a screen lock. If you enable Google Drive backup, your data is protected by your Google account security.

10. Your Rights

Since BirdTRX does not collect your personal data, there is nothing for us to delete or provide on request. Your data belongs entirely to you and lives on your device. You can delete all app data at any time through your device's app settings, or by uninstalling the app.

If you are based in the UK or EU, you have rights under UK GDPR and GDPR respectively. As we do not process your personal data, these rights primarily relate to any correspondence you may send us directly (e.g. support emails).

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this URL. Continued use of BirdTRX after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We will note the date of the most recent update at the top of this page.
